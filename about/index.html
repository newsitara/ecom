Structured Silhouettes.
Zero Compromise.

Every garment begins with yarn selection. Rather than lightweight commercial blends, our fleeces are woven from high-density 520 GSM combed cottons on specialized circular knitting machinery in northern Portugal.

"A great garment holds its shape in space. It doesn't cling; it sculpts an architectural drape."

Pre-shrunk to eliminate post-wash distortion, double-stitched along stress points, and finished with oxidized hardware that patinas uniquely to the wearer.

Horology & Fine Leather Goods.

Our accessories follow the exact same direct-to-client craftsmanship standards. Timepieces housing 28,800 VPH mechanical movements encased in hand-polished 316L surgical steel, paired with Florentine full-grain calfskin leather straps.

No middlemen, no 10x department store markups. Direct atelier delivery to discerning clients worldwide.
